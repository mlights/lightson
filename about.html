Legal & Ownership

The Governance Framework

Top-Level Owner
The LightHouse Family Trust
Trust Structure
Irrevocable dynastic trust designed to hold ownership of LightsOn International across generations. Prevents forced sale or fragmentation. Ensures continuity of control across succession events.
Trust Bank Accounts
The Trust maintains dedicated banking infrastructure separate from operating entities — a key layer of asset protection.
Control Model
Centralized control through LightsOn International. Each operating entity is wholly or majority owned by LOI.
Governance Flow
The LightHouse Family Trust → LightsOn International → Operating Entities. Strategic authority is retained at the LOI level. Operational management may be decentralized.
Voting Control
Maintained at LightsOn International level. Where minority partners exist (e.g. LitNea at 75%), voting rights are governed by operating agreements.
Approval Authority
LightsOn International executive leadership, subject to trust instrument provisions.
Transfer Restrictions
Ownership interests cannot be transferred without LOI governing authority approval. Trust instrument restricts alienation of top-level ownership.

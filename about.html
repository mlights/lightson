Legal & Ownership

The Governance Framework

Top-Level Owner
The Light Trust
Trust Structure
The Light Trust is an irrevocable dynastic trust designed to hold ownership of LightsOn International across generations. It is structured to provide asset protection, prevent forced sale or fragmentation, and ensure continuity of control across succession events.
Control Model
Centralized control through LightsOn International with a minimum 75% ownership requirement in all controlled entities.
Voting Control
Maintained at LightsOn International level. Minority partners have limited voting rights per operating agreements.
Ownership Policy
LightsOn International must own at least 75% of any entity classified as a controlled subsidiary or operating entity within the portfolio.
Approval Authority
LightsOn International executive leadership, subject to trust instrument provisions.
Governance Flow
Governance decisions flow from The Light Trust → LightsOn International → subsidiary entities. Each entity may have its own operational management but strategic authority is retained at the LightsOn International level.
Transfer Restrictions
Ownership interests may not be transferred without approval of LightsOn International governing authority. The trust instrument restricts alienation of top-level ownership.

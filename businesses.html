Ownership Policy

The 75% Standard

LightsOn International maintains a minimum 75% ownership stake in every controlled entity. This is not merely a policy — it is the structural guarantee that strategic authority, operational direction, and long-term value remain concentrated within the group, regardless of any minority partnership arrangements.

Ownership Architecture

Designed for Control & Continuity

Our organizational structure is not accidental — it is a deliberate architecture engineered to protect assets, maintain control across generations, and enable strategic expansion without compromising the integrity of the whole.

  • → The Light Trust holds ultimate beneficial ownership, insulating assets from personal liability and succession disruption.
  • → LightsOn International acts as the central governing body for all portfolio entities.
  • → A minimum 75% ownership threshold in controlled entities ensures strategic authority is never diluted.
  • → Decentralized operations allow each venture to move at market speed while remaining aligned to the group mission.

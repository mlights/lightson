About

The Venture

Brasil Stays is LightsOn International's boldest sustainability play — EV rental bus operations targeting Brazil's enormous and rapidly growing tourism market. It operates as a subsidiary of LitNea, the group's Colombia-based entity.

Brazil presents a compelling opportunity: a $100B+ tourism sector, government incentives for EV adoption, and an underserved premium group transportation segment. Brasil Stays is positioned to capture early-mover advantage in the EV bus rental space.

The $200,000 out-of-pocket investment funds the initial fleet and operational infrastructure. The $1.73M 10-year projection reflects the high unit economics of premium EV bus rentals in a market with strong demand seasonality and year-round tourism.

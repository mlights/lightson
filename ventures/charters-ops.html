About

The Venture

Charters Ops is the capstone of the LightsOn International portfolio — a premium air and sea charter operation targeting South America's luxury travel and corporate markets. It operates as an LLC and subsidiary of AC Rentals.

With a $500,000 startup investment, Charters Ops is the most capital-intensive venture in the portfolio. But the return profile justifies it: a $2.16M 10-year projection makes it the second-highest returning entity behind LightsOn GLOBAI.

The charter market in South America — particularly Colombia, Brazil, and surrounding regions — is underserved by premium operators. Charters Ops positions LightsOn to capture high-margin charter revenue from corporate clients, high-net-worth individuals, and the region's growing luxury tourism sector.

About

The Venture

Juanca Galería is one of the most culturally distinctive entities in the LightsOn portfolio. It combines a curated art gallery with a premium café experience — creating a destination in Colombia that draws artists, collectors, professionals, and the local community.

Structured as a Colombian SAS (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada), Juanca Galería is also referred to as Juanca Art Cafe in the Dynasty financial projections. The SAS structure provides operational flexibility while maintaining full LOI ownership and governance.

The $50,000 startup investment — with a projected $1.08M 10-year return — reflects the strong margin profile of premium hospitality and cultural commerce in Colombia's growing creative economy.

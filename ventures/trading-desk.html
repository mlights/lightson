The Venture

The Trading Desk operates as the Family Office investment arm of The LightHouse Family Trust — distinct from LightsOn International's operating subsidiaries. It handles proprietary trading, capital market exposure, and investment management that feeds returns back into the broader portfolio.

The Trading Desk's 10-year profit projection of $960,000 represents compounded investment returns from a disciplined, rules-based trading operation.
